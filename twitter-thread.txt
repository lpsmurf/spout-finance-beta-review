1/
I spent 4 days inside @SpoutFinance's beta — every endpoint, every flow, real signed transactions with my own wallet.

The pitch: borrow at 0% against tokenized US equities, funded by covered calls. Clever engine.

But their app told me "You own 0.01 GOOG" — and my wallet held zero. 🧵

[IMAGE: screenshots/purchase-confirmed-modal.png]

2/
The mechanism is genuinely well-designed: tokenized shares custodied at Alpaca, an options desk writes covered calls on the pool, and the volatility premium pays lenders 9–32% APY while borrowing stays free.

Docs disclose the loss waterfall plainly. More honest than most DeFi. Credit where due.

3/
But the flagship promise doesn't survive contact with the mechanics: "never trigger a taxable sale."

Their own docs say assigned calls SELL your shares at the strike. That's a sale. That's a taxable event. Auto-Roll rebuys as a new tax lot.

Both can't be true. Fix the headline, not the mechanic.

4/
Over the weekend, the price keeper silently died. For ~53 hours every on-chain action reverted — I signed 3 transactions and watched all fail: OraclePriceStale.

Meanwhile the UI showed "Market: Open", live prices, enabled buy button. Nothing worked. The banner is client-side; it can't see the chain.

5/
Tuesday the keeper recovered. I bought $4 of GOOG through the real UI. Confirmed on-chain. USDC escrowed.

The modal said: "fills at 9:30 AM ET, 22 Sep."

It's now the 23rd. Still pending — 34h past their own promise. $4 in limbo, canceling costs a fee, and the pending-order backlog grew 145 → 268 in a day.

[IMAGE: screenshots/evidence-order-pending.png]

6/
The same modal also said "Your purchase has been confirmed. You own 0.01 GOOG."

My wallet: zero GOOG.

"You own" is a false statement at the moment of highest user trust. If fills fail or price moves, that's a support nightmare waiting to happen.

7/
Smaller footguns found along the way:
• a $1 limit price on a $335 stock passes preflight with zero warnings — funds lock forever
• no rate limiting on tx-build endpoints
• an unauthenticated endpoint leaks any wallet's KYC status
• the leverage slider opens a loan without saying debt, loan, or collateral once

[IMAGE: screenshots/evidence-limit-price.png or evidence-kyc-onboard.png]

8/
What's genuinely good: the vault preflight layer is best-in-class — plain-language blockers with reasons AND remedies. Proof-of-reserves inline at purchase. Declining a signature gives an honest toast, not a raw RPC error. Signature boundary holds; nothing funds-reachable found.

The API layer knows the truth. The UI needs to catch up.
