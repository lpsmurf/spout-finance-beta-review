TWITTER THREAD DRAFT — Spout Finance beta review
(post as a thread; add the repo link in the last post)

1/
I spent 4 days inside @SpoutFinance's beta — every endpoint, every flow, and real signed transactions with my own wallet.

Borrow stablecoins at 0% against tokenized US equities, powered by covered calls. Clever engine.

But I now own a receipt for $4 of stock that was never delivered. 🧵

2/
The mechanism is genuinely well-designed: tokenized shares custodied at Alpaca, an options desk writes covered calls on the pool, and the volatility premium pays lenders (9–32% APY) while making borrowing free.

Docs disclose the loss waterfall plainly. More honest than most DeFi. Credit where due.

3/
But the flagship promise doesn't survive contact with the mechanics: "never trigger a taxable sale."

The docs themselves say assigned covered calls SELL your shares at the strike. That's a sale. That's a taxable event. Auto-Roll rebuys as a new tax lot.

Both things can't be true. Fix the headline, not the mechanic.

4/
The bigger issue is operational. Over the weekend, the price keeper silently died. For ~53 hours, EVERY on-chain action reverted — I know because I signed three transactions and watched them all fail with OraclePriceStale.

Meanwhile the UI showed "Market: Open" with live prices and an enabled buy button.

5/
That banner is computed client-side. It cannot see the chain. "Market: Open" meant the stock market was open — not that Spout could execute.

If this happens on mainnet during a drawdown, positions can't be liquidated OR defended.

6/
Tuesday the keeper recovered. I immediately did a real buy through the UI: $4 USDC → GOOG, confirmed on-chain, USDC escrowed.

It's been 18+ hours. The order never filled. No status, no ETA — and canceling my own stuck order costs a fee.

Pending-order backlog grew 145 → 268 in a day. Nobody's watching the fulfiller either.

7/
Smaller footguns found along the way:
• a $1 limit price on a $353 stock passes preflight with zero warnings — funds lock forever
• no rate limiting on transaction-build endpoints
• the leverage slider performs an invisible loan without saying "borrow" or "debt" once
• "no margin calls" sits directly above a liquidation engine

8/
What's genuinely good: the vault preflight layer is best-in-class — plain-language blockers with reasons AND remedies. Health Factor surfaced. Proof-of-reserves inline at purchase. Security headers tight, signature boundary holds, server simulates before relaying.

Fix the keeper/fulfiller monitoring and sync the marketing with the mechanics, and this is a defensible product.
