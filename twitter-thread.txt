1/10
4 days inside @SpoutFinance's beta: every endpoint, every flow, real signed transactions with my own wallet.

Pitch: borrow at 0% against tokenized US stocks, funded by covered calls. Clever engine.

Until their app said "You own 0.01 GOOG" and my wallet held zero. 🧵

[IMAGE: screenshots/purchase-confirmed-modal.png]

2/10
The mechanism is well-designed: tokenized shares custodied at Alpaca, an options desk writes covered calls on the pool, and the volatility premium pays lenders 9–32% APY while borrowing stays free.

Docs disclose the loss waterfall plainly. More honest than most DeFi.

3/10
The flagship promise doesn't survive its own mechanics: "never trigger a taxable sale."

Their docs: assigned calls SELL your shares at the strike. A sale. A taxable event. Auto-Roll rebuys as a new tax lot.

Both can't be true. Fix the headline, not the mechanic.

4/10
Over the weekend the price keeper silently died. ~53 hours: every on-chain action reverted. I signed 3 transactions and watched all fail — OraclePriceStale.

Meanwhile: "Market: Open", live prices, enabled buy button. The banner is client-side. It can't see the chain.

5/10
Tuesday the keeper recovered. I bought $4 of GOOG through the real UI — confirmed on-chain, USDC escrowed.

The modal promised: "fills at 9:30 AM ET, 22 Sep."

It's now the 23rd. Still pending: 34h past their own promise. Canceling my stuck order costs a fee.

[IMAGE: screenshots/evidence-order-pending.png]

6/10
Not just me: the pending backlog grew 145 → 268 in a day. Nothing is clearing.

And the modal had already declared victory: "Your purchase has been confirmed. You own 0.01 GOOG."

Wallet: zero GOOG. "You own" is false at the highest-trust moment.

7/10
Smaller footguns:
• a $1 limit price on a $335 stock passes preflight with zero warnings — funds lock permanently
• no rate limiting on the tx-build endpoints
• an unauthenticated endpoint leaks any wallet's KYC status

[IMAGE: screenshots/evidence-limit-price.png]

8/10
The leverage slider opens a loan (1.0–2.0x) without saying "debt", "loan", or "collateral" once — and no Health Factor at the decision point.

Meanwhile the marketing says "no margin calls"… directly above a liquidation engine.

9/10
Genuinely good: vault preflights give plain-language blockers with reasons AND remedies. Proof-of-reserves inline at purchase. Declining a signature gives an honest toast.

The API layer knows the truth — the UI needs to catch up. Signature boundary holds.

10/10
